# BeautyStream Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 6, 2026

BeautyStream ("BeautyStream", "we", "our") is a mobile app that lets you broadcast live video from your iPhone to a streaming service of your choice, with optional beauty effects. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## In short

BeautyStream does not collect your personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not receive it.

## What the app accesses on your device

- **Camera and microphone.** Used to show your preview and to capture the video and audio of the livestream you start. The app asks for permission first, and you can change it at any time in iOS Settings.
- **Face effects.** Beauty filters, makeup looks and AR props are applied to the camera image in real time, on your device. Face information used to place an effect is not stored, not sent to us, and not used to identify anyone.

## What leaves your device

Only the live video and audio you choose to broadcast. They are sent directly from your phone to the streaming address you enter in the app (for example a YouTube, Twitch or other RTMP server). We do not operate that server and we do not receive or keep a copy. What the streaming service does with your broadcast is governed by that service's own privacy policy and rules.

## What is stored on your device

- **The last stream address you used**, which includes your stream key, is kept in the iOS Keychain on this device only. It is not sent to us and is not synced to your other devices. You can remove it with "Forget saved stream address" in the app's Settings, or by deleting the app.
- **Your effect choices** (for example the beauty strength you set) are kept in the app's local preferences on this device.

## Third-party software in the app

- **HaishinKit**, an open-source streaming library. It runs on your device and only connects to the address you enter.
- **MeiCam effects SDK**, which provides the beauty filters, makeup looks and AR props. The effects are processed on your device and are not sent to us.

Neither is used to collect data about you.

## Your broadcasts

You decide what you broadcast and where. Please follow the rules of the streaming service you use and the laws that apply to you. Keep your stream key private: anyone who has it can broadcast to your channel.

## Children

BeautyStream does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question about this policy or your privacy, contact us at the address below.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above, and change the app's privacy information in the App Store before the change takes effect.
