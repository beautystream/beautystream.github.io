# BeautyStream Support

BeautyStream lets you broadcast live video from your iPhone to a streaming service, with optional beauty effects. Requires iPhone with iOS 16 or later.

## Contact

Questions, problems or feedback: tp26610@gmail.com

When you write, please tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing. Please **never** send us your stream key.

## Getting started

1. Open BeautyStream and allow camera and microphone access.
2. Get your stream address and stream key from the streaming service you use.
   - **YouTube:** in YouTube Studio, start a live stream and find the Stream URL (it looks like `rtmp://a.rtmp.youtube.com/live2`) and the Stream key.
   - Other services show a server URL and a key in the same way.
3. In BeautyStream, paste the stream URL, then a slash, then the stream key, for example `rtmp://a.rtmp.youtube.com/live2/your-stream-key`
4. Tap Go live. The status at the top left shows Connecting, then LIVE with a timer. While you are live the address field is hidden so your stream key is not shown on screen.

Keep your stream key private. Anyone who has it can broadcast to your channel.

## Beauty effects

Tap the Beauty button (the sparkles icon on the right), then turn on the Beauty switch to adjust Smooth and Whiten, and to pick a filter, an AR prop or a makeup look. You can change them while you are live. Tap an active effect again to turn it off. Effects work best with your face clearly in view.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why did my stream end when I left the app?**
BeautyStream ends the broadcast when it leaves the foreground (for example when you press Home, lock the screen or switch apps), because iOS stops the camera in the background. Open the app and tap Go live again. The app keeps your screen awake while you are live.

**The status says Reconnecting.**
The connection dropped. The app tries to reconnect up to five times. If it cannot, it shows Not live. Check your internet connection and the stream address, then tap Go live.

**It says Permission needed.**
Allow camera and microphone access in iOS Settings, then return to the app. The Open Settings button takes you there.

**The effects do not show.**
Make sure the Beauty switch is turned on and your face is in view. If the effects cannot start on your phone, the app shows a message and the broadcast continues without them.

**Can I mute my microphone?**
Yes. Tap Mute. The stream continues with no sound.

**How do I remove my saved stream address?**
Open Settings (the gear icon) and tap Forget saved stream address.

**Does BeautyStream work on iPad?**
Not yet. The app is made for iPhone.
